# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and
experience level, and focus criticism on ideas and code, never on people. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at mahim.devx@gmail.com. Every report is
reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break this code, and may ban repeat offenders from the project.
